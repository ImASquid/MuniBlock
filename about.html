About MuniBlock

Transparency Through Technology

Founded in San Marcos, Texas, to help local governments navigate the complexities of modern public finance using the most secure technology available today.

Our Origin

MuniBlock was founded with a single goal: give city, county, and state governments a modern, verifiable way to manage public money. We saw the same story repeat across municipalities — siloed departments, delayed audits, and taxpayers left to simply trust that funds were spent correctly.

The Advantage

By removing the silos between departments, we create a single "source of truth" that makes auditing easier and reduces the cost of issuing public debt. Every transaction — from a vendor payment to a bond issuance — is recorded once, verified continuously, and impossible to alter quietly.
